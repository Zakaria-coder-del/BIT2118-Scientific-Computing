## TASK A: NumPy Arrays and Numerical Computation

### 1. Modified Real-World Problem
A weather station records rainfall measurements over seven days. NumPy is used to analyse the rainfall and convert measurements from millimetres to centimetres.

### 2. Input Values
The rainfall measurements are:

[0, 2.5, 10.2, 0, 0, 5.1, 12.7] mm

### 3. Mathematical Operations
- **Mean:** Calculates the average rainfall.
- **Minimum:** Finds the lowest rainfall.
- **Maximum:** Finds the highest rainfall.
- **Sum:** Calculates total rainfall.
- **Division by 10:** Converts millimetres into centimetres.

### 4. Output and Interpretation
- Mean rainfall: 4.36 mm
- Minimum rainfall: 0 mm
- Maximum rainfall: 12.70 mm
- Total rainfall: 30.50 mm

The results show that the highest rainfall was 12.7 mm, while some days recorded no rainfall.

### 5. Changing an Input
If the rainfall measurement of 12.7 mm is changed to 20 mm, the total and average rainfall increase. The maximum rainfall also increases to 20 mm.

### 6. Conclusion
NumPy makes numerical calculations easier and faster by performing operations on entire arrays without requiring individual calculations.
